Optional analytics is off by default. Daily Dividend loads Google Analytics or PostHog only after you accept analytics. Necessary authentication and billing storage remains available so the service can work.

1. About cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small text file stored by your browser. Local storage is a similar browser feature that can keep a preference or identifier on your device. We use both, and this policy refers to them together as browser storage where convenient.

First-party storage is set for Daily Dividend's domain. Some optional tools then send information to their providers. Your browser may shorten or block the durations described below.

2. Necessary and functional storage

This storage supports account access, security, the Google sign-in exchange, subscriptions, consent records and interface preferences. It is not used for advertising.

4. External resources

The application requests fonts from Google Fonts, emoji assets from jsDelivr, and some company images from Daily Dividend's server, which may obtain them from Logo.dev. Loading an external resource normally exposes connection data such as IP address and browser headers to that provider. These resources are used to render the interface rather than to run Daily Dividend advertising profiles.

Google sign-in is used only when you choose it. Stripe-hosted pages are opened only when you start checkout or manage billing.

5. Manage or withdraw your choices

On your first relevant visit, choose Accept all, Reject optional or Manage preferences. Optional analytics is never preselected. You can change your mind at any time:

When you withdraw analytics consent, Daily Dividend disables future Google Analytics and PostHog capture in the current browser and removes common first-party analytics cookies and persistence where accessible. Provider records already lawfully collected are handled under the retention and rights sections of the Privacy Policy.

You can also delete cookies and local storage through browser settings. Blocking necessary storage may prevent sign-in, saved preferences or payment flows from working correctly.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when browser storage or providers change. If a change introduces a new optional purpose, we will request a new choice where required. The effective date above identifies this version.
