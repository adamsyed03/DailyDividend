Daily Dividend
The chip squeeze

Only a few companies make what Claude runs on

Its most advanced chips are made mostly by TSMC in Taiwan. The high-bandwidth memory inside them comes mostly from just three firms: SK hynix, Samsung and Micron.

When supply is tight, prices rise. Last quarter Anthropic had to buy $5bn of short-term computing power at a premium, which is a big reason it slipped back to a loss.

Fun fact: TSMC and Micron are both buying shares in the IPO.
