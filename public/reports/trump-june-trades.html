The one thing to remember

Buying a name is not the same as picking it.

Direct indexing buys hundreds of individual companies to copy an index, then sells losers to cut a tax bill. It produces exactly this: enormous volume, individual names, and the same stock bought and sold within days.

694 of 1,051were filed after the legal deadline

The real question is not whether Trump likes Visa. It is why the accounts of a sitting president traded rocket, missile and intelligence companies paid by his own government.
