Two words you need

Read the headline carefully

Schneider alone explains most of the drop from about $50bn to $42bn. That is one cheque coming in, not conviction going out.

The one thing to remember

They are not trading India. They are owning it.

They bought the stock exchange in 2010 and are only selling now. Sixteen years. They held three of that month's four IPOs while other investors cashed out.

The signal is not what they buy. It is how long they refuse to sell.
