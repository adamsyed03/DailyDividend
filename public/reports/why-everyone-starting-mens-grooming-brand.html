    10 · DD take

    A famous face gets you the first sale.

    Education, not advice. DD maths are our own estimates. Sources: Euromonitor, Redseer × Fireside Ventures, Aditya Birla Capital, Groww, Philips India, Hamilton Sciences, company and exchange filings, Entrackr, Inc42.

    Stars can get men to try a new face wash once. What brings them back is a product they can't do without, like Gillette's blades or Muuchstac's one face wash. Most celebrity brands haven't shown that yet.
