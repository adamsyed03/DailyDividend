01 · The mega listings

NSE

Moving

The company that owns the stock market.

What they do

NSE runs the market itself: equities, derivatives and the clearing house that settles every trade.

How they make money

A tiny fee on every trade. That is essentially the entire business.

The pattern

Two things nobody is writing about.

01 · The same funds keep appearing

Temasek owns pieces of Haldiram's and Cult.fit. ADIA, Mubadala, IHC and KKR recur across the list. Retail investors are being offered a seat as sovereign funds look to sell down.

02 · Almost nobody is raising money

NSE, PhonePe and Kent RO are complete exits. Much of OYO's raise repays a loan; most of Cult.fit's pays rent. Jio, Zepto and Snapdeal are the exceptions.

Ask this every time: is the company raising money to grow, or is somebody raising money to leave?
