06 · The hidden truth

Reliance started by selling spices from a 350 sq ft room.

Dhirubhai Ambani worked at a petrol pump in Aden, Yemen. In 1958 he came home and rented a room in Masjid Bunder with one table and three chairs, exporting spices and importing polyester yarn. Today the same company runs the world's largest refinery.

11 · The DD take

Priced like an oil company. Built like more.

Education, not advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold. Sources: RIL Q2 FY27 results (30 Oct 2026), TRAI, NSE shareholding (Sep 2026), CompaniesMarketCap (Nov 2026), Ormax, company filings. DD maths are our own estimates.

For five years the stock went nowhere while the businesses kept growing. This quarter was a real beat, but about ₹85 of every ₹100 of extra profit came from war-boosted oil margins, which can fade as quickly as they came. What decides the next five years is quieter: whether Jio can keep raising prices and whether retail gets back to growth. If they start carrying the numbers, there could be a real re-rating.

Spices to everything

How a trading room became India's biggest company

1958

Spices and yarn. Dhirubhai starts trading from a 350 sq ft room in Masjid Bunder.

1966

Textiles. First mill, and the Vimal brand.

1977

Goes public. Thousands of ordinary Indians buy their first shares.

1980s

Polyester, then chemicals. It starts making the raw materials behind its own cloth.

1999

Jamnagar opens. Today it's the world's largest single refinery.

2005

The split. Mukesh and Anil divide the empire. Telecom goes to Anil.

2006

Retail. The first Reliance Fresh store opens.

2010

Back to telecom. Reliance buys Infotel, the only company to win 4G airwaves across India.

2016

Jio launches with free calls and data.

2020

Meta and Google buy into Jio.

2024

JioStar. Merges with Disney's Star India.

2026

Jio IPO, India's biggest ever.
