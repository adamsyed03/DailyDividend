05 · Indriya

A design-first jeweller chasing Indian weddings.

It sells gold, diamond and polki jewellery, and about half its sales come from weddings. In its first year it lost ₹332 crore, which is 47% of its sales, because new stores and stock cost a lot upfront.

13 · DD take

A family that sells to you needs a face you know.

Education, not advice. DD maths are rough estimates. Sources: company results, Svatantra's DRHP (Aug 2026), Grasim filings and news reports.

The Birlas are spending heavily to become a name you know, and for now their old cement and metal businesses are paying for it. Only a few of the new brands make money so far. Ananya being everywhere is part of that plan, because a family that sells to people needs a face people recognise.
