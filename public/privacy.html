This policy reflects the data flows found in the Daily Dividend application as of its effective date. It does not treat acceptance of our Terms as consent to unrelated processing, and optional analytics remains subject to a separate choice.

1. Data controller

Daily Dividend Ltd is the controller of personal information processed to provide and improve the Daily Dividend service, except where a provider acts as a separate controller for its own purposes. Daily Dividend Ltd is incorporated in England and Wales.

To complete before publication: add the verified registered office, company number and privacy contact. These details were not present in the repository and have not been invented.

2. Personal information we collect

Account and profile information

Name, email address, username, country, phone country code and number, profession, finance-proficiency and investor-level responses, age range, how you heard about us, and your selected market and sector interests.

Authentication information

Supabase account identifiers, sign-in method, session tokens and security information needed to authenticate the account. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth; Daily Dividend's application database does not store your raw password. Google provides identity details when you choose Google sign-in.

Membership and billing information

Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, subscription status, cancellation status, current billing-period end, checkout status, and limited billing contact information returned by Stripe. We do not receive or store your complete payment-card number or security code.

Product activity and preferences

Stories read, saved companies, market calls and votes, reading streaks, search and feature interactions, selected interests, and derived engagement summaries used to operate your shelf and personalise the product.

Technical and analytics information

Browser and device information, IP-derived or approximate location, referring page, pages and features used, timestamps, diagnostics, and similar online identifiers. Daily Dividend does not currently maintain a separate application access-log database, but its hosting, authentication, security and analytics providers may process technical logs.

Communications and submitted content

Information you send if you contact support, together with any content or feedback you submit through a feature that accepts it.

3. How we collect information

We collect information directly from you during registration and onboarding, automatically when you use the website, from Google if you choose Google sign-in, from Stripe when you start or manage a subscription, and from our service providers when they help us operate or secure the service.

Some account preferences are also kept in your browser's local storage so the interface can restore your state. See the Cookie Policy.

5. Payment processing and Stripe

Stripe processes subscription checkout, payment methods, recurring charges, promotion codes, invoices and the customer billing portal. Stripe receives the payment details and billing information you enter on its hosted pages. Stripe may act as our processor for some services and as an independent controller where it determines its own legal and regulatory processing.

Daily Dividend receives identifiers and operational details needed to link the membership to your account: the Stripe customer and subscription IDs, checkout completion, payment or subscription status, cancellation status and paid-access end date. Full card details do not pass through our application server.

Read Stripe's Privacy Policy.

6. Supabase and authentication

Supabase provides authentication, database services and file storage used by Daily Dividend. It processes account identities and sessions and stores application state under our configuration. Google OAuth is connected through Supabase when you choose Google sign-in.

Authentication uses secure, HTTP-only cookies set by the Daily Dividend server. Temporary PKCE and return-context cookies support the Google OAuth exchange and expire after a short period. Read Supabase's Privacy Policy.

7. Analytics, cookies and similar technologies

With your permission, Daily Dividend uses Google Analytics 4 on the login experience and PostHog product analytics in the signed-in application. They can process online identifiers, device and browser details, approximate location, pages viewed and feature interactions. PostHog may also receive the internal user ID and profile attributes such as country, profession, investor level and reading streak when you are identified in the app.

Neither analytics tool is loaded until you accept analytics in our consent controls. Rejecting analytics does not prevent account, payment or reading features from working. No Meta Pixel loader was present in the audited application; a dormant event helper does not send information without the Meta script.

For exact browser-storage details and durations, read the Cookie Policy or open .

8. Service messages, newsletters and marketing

Daily Dividend may send essential account or billing messages needed to authenticate you, confirm a payment, provide a receipt, warn of a security issue, or communicate a material service or contract change. These are not optional marketing messages.

The audited application does not currently operate a marketing newsletter or store a marketing-consent preference. If optional promotional email is introduced, we will keep that choice separate from account creation, provide the required information when collecting it, and include an unsubscribe method. We will not treat agreement to the Terms or acknowledgement of this policy as marketing consent.

10. International data transfers

Our providers may process information in the United Kingdom, European Economic Area, United States and other countries. PostHog is configured to use its EU endpoint. Provider locations and subprocessors can change.

Where UK personal information is transferred to a country without UK adequacy regulations, we will use an applicable safeguard such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to approved standard contractual clauses, or another lawful mechanism, and assess supplementary measures where required. Provider contracts, regions and transfer documents must be verified operationally; this policy does not claim that an unverified safeguard has been signed.

11. How long we keep information

We retain account and product data while your account is active and afterwards only for as long as reasonably needed to close the account, meet accounting and legal obligations, handle disputes, prevent fraud and maintain necessary records. Stripe, Supabase, Google and PostHog apply their own configured retention periods to data they host.

Verified browser-storage periods are listed in the Cookie Policy. We do not publish an invented fixed deletion schedule for application records. Before launch, Daily Dividend should approve and document an internal retention schedule covering account deletion, backups, billing records, support communications and analytics.

12. Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law gives you rights to:

  • ask for access to your personal information and information about its use;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request deletion or restriction of processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests or to direct marketing;
  • receive certain information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier lawful processing; and
  • ask for safeguards relating to a significant decision made solely by automated means, if applicable.

To exercise a right, use the verified privacy contact to be added in section 16. We may need to verify your identity. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concern first, but this is not a condition of contacting the ICO.

13. Security

Implemented measures include HTTP-only authentication cookies with SameSite controls, server-side storage of Stripe and Supabase secret keys, Stripe webhook-signature verification, authenticated billing endpoints, access controls, and HTTPS in production. We limit the payment data that enters Daily Dividend's systems by using Stripe-hosted checkout.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. You should use a strong, unique password and protect access to your email and device.

14. Children

Daily Dividend's registration experience is designed for people aged 18 or over and does not offer an under-18 onboarding option. The service is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child through account registration. If you believe a child has created an account, use the privacy contact to be added below.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our service, providers or legal obligations change. We will publish the updated version and effective date. If a change materially affects how we use existing personal information, we will provide additional notice or request a new choice where required.
